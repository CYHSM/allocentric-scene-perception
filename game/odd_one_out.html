After the Four Mountains Task (Hartley et al. 2007; Chan et al. 2016), an established clinical measure of allocentric spatial perception. All images are rendered in Blender Cycles raytracing from a strictly fixed circular orbit (17° elevation, R = 94.0) around the central tarn, maintaining exact scale while revolving 360°. Foils retain the identical 4 landforms (Matterhorn, Knife-edge ridge, Tableland mesa, and Volcanic dome) and vary only their allocentric spatial configuration.
